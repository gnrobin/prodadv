Northwind Data Platform

Northwind is licensed per seat. Every user is a Creator, an Explorer, or a Viewer, and the per-seat rate steps down as total seat count grows. Modules attach to licensed seats only. Support is priced as a percentage of software with a floor. This tab is the published rate card for reference — build a live quote on Configure Quote.
