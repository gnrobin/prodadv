How I Work

Every engagement runs the same three beats, at whatever scale the work requires.

Step 1

Collaborate

A listening tour first: stakeholder interviews, reverse demos, sitting in on pipeline reviews. I learn your motion before I have an opinion about it.

Step 2

Align

Current state written down, problems separated from symptoms, priorities ranked against your team’s real capacity. Leadership signs off on the order of operations.

Step 3

Build

I build with your team, not for them. We pair on the hard configuration, document as we go, and I coach your people to own and extend it.

“Organizations that want the artifact without doing the alignment are asking for a lie that reports well.”

Leadership will call Salesforce strategic, then run the Monday sales meeting off a spreadsheet someone rebuilt Sunday night. That gap isn’t a training problem or a data problem. It’s a system that was configured before anyone agreed on how the team actually runs.

A change mindset, not a slogan. Whether you’re putting in a new system or revamping an existing one, the goal isn’t to go through the motions. It’s to be purposeful about getting somewhere better. Collaborate, Align, Build gets stakeholders bought in early, so when the system rolls out, the change is directed and owned by the people who have to live with it.
