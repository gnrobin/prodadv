How I Work

Every engagement runs the same three beats, at whatever scale the work requires.

Collaborate. A listening tour first — stakeholder interviews, reverse demos, sitting in on pipeline reviews. I understand your motion before I have an opinion about it.
Align. Current state written down, problems separated from symptoms, priorities ranked by impact against your team’s real capacity. Leadership signs off on the order of operations.
Build. I implement with your team, not for them — pairing on the hard configurations, documenting as we go, and coaching them to own and extend it.

The order matters because that’s exactly where these systems break down. Leadership will tell you Salesforce is strategic, then run the Monday sales meeting off a spreadsheet someone rebuilt Sunday night. That gap isn’t a training problem or a data problem — it’s a system that was configured before anyone agreed on how the team actually runs. Skip the collaborate step and you build the wrong thing well. Skip the align step and you build the right thing for one stakeholder while three others keep their workarounds. Adoption isn’t something you drive after go-live; it’s decided in the two steps before the build.
